Paprika Cream Chickenbreast Recipe for the Oven

Home

Description:

Tasty and protein-rich chickenbreast with paprika and cream sauce.

For 4 servings.

Ingredients

  • 4 chickenbreast fillets
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 green bell pepper
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 dried chili pepper
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 tsp tomato paste
  • 125ml vegetable broth
  • 1 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp hot paprika
  • 100g grated cheese
  • Oil for cooking
  • Salt to taste
  • Paprika to taste

Preparation

Total time: 1h | Prep time: 20min | Cooking/baking time: 40min |

  1. Preheat your oven to 180 °C (top/bottom heat).
  2. Wash and pat the chicken fillets dry with paper towels.
    Season both sides with salt and sweet paprika, then place them closely side by side in a baking dish.
  3. Wash the bell peppers and remove the seeds.
    Cut them into thin strips and spread them evenly over the chicken fillets.
  4. Cut the onion into half-rings and cook in a pan with a little vegetable oil until translucent.
    Finely chop or crumble the chili, press the garlic cloves, and add both to the pan.
  5. Stir in the paprika and tomato paste, then add the vegetable broth to the pan and let it simmer briefly.
  6. Lower the heat, stir in the heavy cream and sour cream, and season with salt to taste.
  7. Pour the sauce evenly over the chicken and pepper strips so everything is well covered.
  8. Sprinkle the grated cheese on top.
  9. Bake uncovered on the middle rack for about 30 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the cheese is melted and golden brown.

Serving Suggestions

  • Pasta: Serve alongside ribbon noodles (tagliatelle) to soak up the sauce.
  • Rice: A side of steamed white rice pairs well with the creamy paprika flavor.
