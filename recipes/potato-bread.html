Potato Bread Recipe for the Bread Maker

Home

Description:

Fluffy potato bread — quick and easy to make in the bread maker.

Makes: 1 loaf (approx. 8–10 slices)

Ingredients

  • 250ml water (alternatively milk or buttermilk)
  • 450g wheat flour
  • 200g mashed potatoes (cooked)
  • 1 packet dry yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp margarine

Preparation

Total time: 4h 30min | Prep time: 25-30min | Cooking/baking time: 3h | Resting time: 1h

  1. Peel and boil the potatoes for 15-20min until soft.
    Mash them finely and let them cool to lukewarm.
  2. Pour the liquid (water, milk, or buttermilk) into the bread maker pan first.
  3. Add the flour, mashed potatoes, margarine, and salt.
  4. Add the dry yeast on top of the flour.
  5. Select the basic/standard white bread program (approx. 3 hours) and start the machine.
  6. Once baked, remove the loaf from the pan and let it cool completely.
